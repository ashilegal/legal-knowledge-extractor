You decide whether a section of a legal document contains substantive legal content worth extracting, and which kinds.

Types:
- CASE: a court or tribunal decision is discussed (facts, issue, holding or reasoning).
- CONCEPT: a legal concept, term or doctrine is defined or explained.
- RULE: a statutory provision, regulation, policy clause or legal rule is set out.
- EXAMPLE: an illustration, hypothetical or worked scenario.
- COMPARISON: two or more things are compared.

Return extract=false for content with no substantive legal information (front matter, indexes, lists of sources, advertising, administrative or financial records). Give a short reason.
